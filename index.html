Problem Statement

  • Reactive Systems: Traditional monitoring relies on manual sampling or static thresholds.
  • No Predictive Insight: Fails to capture complex temporal dynamics of water quality.
  • Hidden Nitrogen Risks: Ammonia & Nitrate are treated as threshold alerts, catching toxicity only *after* it becomes lethal.
  • No Lead Time: Lack of decision-support systems leaves operators without time to react.

"Current systems detect failure. They do not prevent it."

Key Contributions & Novelty

Our system moves beyond isolated parameter tracking by introducing a Physics-Guided, Cascaded Prediction Architecture.

  • Multivariate LSTM: Joint modeling of DO, Temp, pH, and Turbidity (capturing interactions).
  • Cascaded Nitrogen Model: Uses physical parameter forecasts to predict chemical (Ammonia/Nitrate) stress dynamically.
  • Physics-Guided Stabilization: Post-processing predictions using real-world ecological constraints (e.g., thermal inertia, DO saturation).
  • Decision-Oriented Outputs: Translates raw numerical forecasts into calculated risk scores and intervention lead-times.

  • Feature Engineering:

    • → Output Forecasting Horizon ($H$): 6 future steps

Physics-Guided Stabilization Layer

Neural networks can output physically impossible predictions. We enforce ecological constraints via a post-processing layer:

Thermal Inertia Correction

Restricts abrupt temperature changes, mimicking the slow thermal response of large water bodies.

DO Reaeration Bounds

Forces predicted oxygen levels toward natural saturation equilibrium limits.

pH-Ammonia Coupling

Enforces known chemical interactions between predicted pH and predicted Ammonia toxicity.

Soft Ecological Bounds

$x_{pred} = min(max(x_{pred}, x_{min}), x_{max})$ ensures biologically feasible ranges.

Risk Assessment & Early Warning

Converts raw numerical forecasts into a weighted composite risk score:

R = (W_DO × r_DO) + (W_Temp × r_Temp) + (W_pH × r_pH) + (W_NH3 × r_NH3) + (W_NO3 × r_NO3)

Calculates Lead Time: The exact forecast step where conditions first enter the 'Critical' state.

Implementation Challenges

  • Sensor Noise & Dropped Packets: Real-world IoT deployments yielded fragmented time-series data, forcing the creation of the dual XGBoost/RF Imputation layer.
  • Physical Violations in ML: Deep learning models occasionally predicted impossible physical states (e.g., pH > 14). Resolved by building the Hybrid Stabilization Layer.
  • Model Tuning: Balancing the Lookback window ($L=48$) and Horizon ($H=6$). A larger horizon reduced accuracy exponentially, requiring hyperparameter optimization.

Future Enhancements
